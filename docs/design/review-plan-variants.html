Lumen · review-triage · design round 1

Review plan pane

Three ways to show the tiered plan for andrew-crm#58. Each is drawn at 1280×800 and 1920×1080 with Lumen's own colour tokens; flip your system theme to see dark. The summary, tiers and reasons are the real output of the triage engine for that pull request, with two illustrative groups added so group rows are visible.

Recommendation

A, the plan in the right pane. It replaces the flat Review focus list in place, keeps the diff at full width, and reads top to bottom in the same order J and K walk. Cost: at 1280 the pane is 344 wide, so long group titles wrap to two lines, and precedent and patterns move below the plan instead of beside the current point.

Variant A

Plan in the right pane

The right pane becomes the plan: summary and progress, then Critical, Worth a look and Skim open, Skip and Consistency folded. Groups sit in their tier as rows with a hunk count and a check; review points keep their existing row. The file tree and diff are untouched.

Variant B

Plan as a tab in the left pane

The left pane gets a Plan / Files switch and opens on Plan. The right pane keeps the current point's precedent and patterns, so detail and plan are both visible. Fits the review order to the file-shaped column people already scan.

Variant C

Plan board before the diff

Opening a pull request shows the plan as four tier columns across the centre. Pressing J or picking an item drops into the diff with a slim plan strip on top. Strongest overview of the whole pull request, at the cost of a mode switch.

Rejected on the way

A score or confidence per item, because tiers carry reasons in words. Hiding Skip entirely, because proven-mechanical code should stay one click away. Colouring every row by tier, because colour stays reserved for severity and the one accent.
